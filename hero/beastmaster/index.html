Изменения в патче 7.42

Общее

  • Agility gain increased from 2.0 to 2.3
  • Damage gain per level increased from 3.1 to 3.2
  • Removed Drums of Slom ability

Wild Axes

  • Damage per axe increased from 40/80/120/160 to 45/85/125/165
  • Damage Amp per stack decreased from 5/6/7/8% to 4/5/6/7%

Summon Raptors

  • Cooldown decreased from 30s to 25s
  • Dive Damage increased from 60/95/150/165 to 70/110/150/190

Summon Ancient Thunderhide

  • New ability granted with Aghanim's Scepter
  • Active. No target
  • Mana Cost: 100. Cooldown: 60s. Cast Point: 0.3s
  • Summons an Ancient Thunderhide to fight along with Beastmaster. It has a Slam ability that slows nearby enemies and a passive Thunderhide Drum Aura that increases max health and damage of Beastmaster and his controlled units. Ancient Thunderhide is considered a Creep-Hero and can not be resummoned if one is currently alive and took damage within the last 3 seconds
  • Thunderhide Health: 2200. Mana: 400
  • Armor: 7. Magic Resistance: 35%
  • Damage: 90. Movement Speed: 355. Attack Range: 525
  • XP Bounty: 124. Gold Bounty: 175
  • Slam: Active. No Target. Magical Damage. Doesn't pierce Debuff Immunity. Dispellable with any Dispels
  • Mana Cost: 50. Cooldown: 15s. Cast Point: 0.3s
  • The Ancient Thunderhide slams his mammoth foot against the ground 3 times. Shockwaves decrease with each slam, damage nearby enemies by 175 damage and slow their movement by 50% and attack by 50. Duration is 3s for heroes and 4s for other units. Radius: 550 - 100 with each slam. Slam interval: 0.3s
  • This ability starts on a 5s cooldown when Thunderhide is summoned
  • Thunderhide Drum: Passive. Aura. Affects the owner and their controlled units only. Cannot be dispelled
  • Whenever Ancient Thunderhide, its owner or other unit they control within the aura make an attack, the ability gains a stack. Each stack increases the max health and base damage of all affected units. If no attacks are made, Ancient Thunderhide dies or a unit leaves the aura radius, stacks are gradually lost, 1 per second. Also grants 1 stack per Slam's wave that hit an enemy hero
  • Aura Radius: 1200. Max Stacks: 20. Max HP and Base Damage per stack: 1%
  • The stack-losing timer starts immediately after the last attack was made
  • Hitting multiple heroes with a single Slam wave still grants only 1 stack

Таланты

  • Level 20 Talent Health to Beastmaster and his summons increased from +250 to +300
  • Level 20 Talent Damage to Beastmaster and his summons increased from +25 to +30
