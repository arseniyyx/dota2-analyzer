Изменения в патче 7.42

Zealot

  • Ability Reworked
  • Has both passive and active components
  • Passive. Can't be Broken
  • Increases movespeed, base damage and spell damage of creeps persuaded and dominated by Chen, and sets a minimum threshold for their max health. Each creep gains a special ability that lets Chen anoint this unit as his Zealot. Anointed Zealot is considered a creep-hero, gains additional base attack damage, has Martyrdom ability and can be resurrected. Chen can have only 1 Anointed Zealot at a time, and starts the game with a melee lane creep as a Zealot
  • Anointed Zealot's Bonus Base Damage: 2 per hero level
  • Controlled Base and Spell Damage Amp: 5% + 2% per hero level
  • Controlled Bonus Movespeed: 5 + 2 per hero level
  • Controlled Minimum Health: 400 + 50 per hero level
  • Active. No-targeted
  • No Mana Cost. Cooldown: 60s - 1s per hero level
  • Resurrects the anointed Zealot
  • Martyrdom is unchanged

Penitence

  • Damage increased from 50/100/150/200 to 60/120/180/240

Holy Persuasion

  • No longer provides bonus damage or movement speed to persuaded creeps
  • Creep's min health is now set by Zealot innate ability
  • Chen may now target himself to teleport all his controlled creeps to himself after a 6/5/4/3 second delay. This places the ability on a 3s cooldown.

Таланты

  • Level 15 Talent +12% Holy Persuasion Damage replaced with +12% Zealot Damage/Spell Amp
  • Level 20 Talent +1300 Holy Persuasion Minimum Health changed to +1300 Zealot Minimum Health
