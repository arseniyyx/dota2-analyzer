Изменения в патче 7.42

Общее

  • Base attack speed increased from 100 to 110

Bone Chill

  • No longer slows by default

Cold Feet

  • Now applies a 4/6/8/10% movement slow

Ice Vortex

  • Movement Slow increased from 8% to 10/15/20/25%
  • Increased Magic Damage decreased from 16/19/22/25% to 14/16/18/20%

Chilling Touch

  • Mana Cost decreased from 35 to 30
  • Damage increased from 35/65/95/125 to 40/80/120/160

Ice Blast

  • Impact Damage decreased from 250/325/450 to 200/300/400
  • Release sub-ability can now be cast during channeled spells

Таланты

  • Level 10 Talent +30 Cold Feet Damage Per Second replaced with +150 Cold Feet Break Distance
  • Level 15 Talent +250 Cold Feet Break Distance replaced with +65 Chilling Touch Damage
  • Level 15 Talent -2s Ice Vortex Cooldown replaced with Bone Chill Slows by 2% (the slow also works on creeps)
  • Level 20 Talent +100 Chilling Touch Damage replaced with Cold Feet Leashes
