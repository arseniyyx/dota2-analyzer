Изменения в патче 7.42

Общее

  • Base Intelligence increased from 19 to 20
  • Damage at level 1 unchanged at 43-49
  • Intelligence gain decreased from 3.6 to 3.5
  • Damage gain per level decreased from +3.2 to +3.1

Malefice

  • Aghanim's Shard no longer spawns Eidolons when triggering on non-heroes

Demonic Summoning

  • Eidolon health regeneration decreased from 4 to 1/2/3/4
  • Eidolon damage decreased from 12-20/24-32/36-44/48-56 to 12-18/24-30/36-42/48-54
  • Average damage decreased from 16/28/40/52 to 15/27/39/51, but also the damage spread is decreased from 8 to 6

Black Hole

  • Damage per second decreased from 100/150/200 to 90/135/180
  • Level 10 Talent Black Hole Damage Per Second decreased from +50 to +40
  • As a part of the comprehensive Spell Power Reduction
