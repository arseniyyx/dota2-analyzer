Изменения в патче 7.42

Общее

  • Removed Swiftslash ability

Blade Fury

  • Juggernaut is now fully disarmed during Blade Fury
  • Now applies Basic dispel at the end of the spell instead of a Strong one
  • Now upgraded with Aghanim's Scepter
  • Allows Juggernaut to attack his enemies during Blade Fury even while moving. These attacks deal 75% damage and Item Potency
  • Keeps attacking units even if the stop/hold command is issued and while stunned. Buildings are not automatically attacked
  • If no attack command is issued, attacks the closest enemy unit first

Таланты

  • Level 20 Talent +120 Blade Fury Damage Per Second replaced with -3s Blade Fury Cooldown
