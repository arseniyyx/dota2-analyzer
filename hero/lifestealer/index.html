Изменения в патче 7.42

Infest

  • Self Max HP Regen decreased from 3/4/5% to 2/3/4%
  • Aghanim's Scepter Self Max HP Regen decreased from 6/8/10% to 4/6/8%
  • Damage Radius decreased from 700 to 600
  • Aghanim's Shard now also applies a 3s Open Wounds effect to any enemies affected by Infest explosion
  • This effect applies to any Infest explosion, while damage over time still applies only on creep consumption
  • Aghanim's Scepter can no longer target Debuff Immune enemies and no longer ignores spell reflection
