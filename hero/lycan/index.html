Изменения в патче 7.42

Общее

  • Removed Apex Predator innate ability

Pack Hunter

  • New Innate Ability
  • Passive. Breakable. Affects Lycan and his controlled units. Physical Damage. Doesn't pierce Debuff Immunity. Cannot be dispelled
  • Lycan and units under his control mark enemies on attack, revealing them even if invisible or in fog of war, and have increased damage and lifesteal against marked units. Effect stacks, but each unit may apply only 1 mark per enemy
  • Applying mark to a new enemy does not remove it from the previous target
  • Mark Duration: 6s; Lifesteal: 3% + 1% per 3 levels; Bonus Damage: 5%

Wolf Bite

  • No longer provides lifesteal when Lycan and the target are near each other
  • While shapeshifted, the target counts as a unit under Lycan's control for Pack Hunter

Таланты

  • Level 20 Talent +25% Feral Impulse Damage replaced with +2% Pack Hunter Bonus Damage Per Stack
