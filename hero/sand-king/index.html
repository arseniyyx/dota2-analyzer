Изменения в патче 7.42

Burrowstrike

  • Now upgraded with Aghanim's Scepter
  • Increases Cast Range by 425 (to 975/1050/1125/1200). Sand King leaves behind a trail of spines, that, after a 0.8s delay, create Stinger attack areas along the path, that apply 75% of Stinger damage to enemies hit
  • Each Stinger attack has the same bonus as the innermost area

Stinger

  • Now has 60% Item Potency
  • Bonus Damage increased from 50/75/100/125 to 50/80/110/140
  • Inner radius rescaled from 125 to 120/140/160/180

Epicenter

  • No longer upgraded with Aghanim's Scepter

Таланты

  • Level 15 Talent +200 Burrowstrike Cast Range replaced with -15s Epicenter Cooldown
