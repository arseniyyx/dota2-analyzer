Изменения в патче 7.42

Ceaseless Dirge

  • Is no longer affected by cooldown reduction

Tombstone

  • No longer upgraded with Aghanim's Shard

Flesh Golem

  • Damage Amplification decreased from 25/30/35% to 20/25/30%

Zombie Bite

  • New Ability granted by Aghanim's Shard
  • Active. Physical damage. Doesn't pierce Debuff Immunity. Dispellable with Strong Dispel only.
  • Cast Range: Attack Range + 250 (400 total). Mana Cost: 70. Cooldown: 18s
  • Undying lunges at the target and sinks his teeth in it, landing an attack with damage increased by 250 and healing Undying for the damage dealt. Biting a hero will turn them into a silenced melee zombie that is slowed by 25% and deals 40% less damage for 3s. Biting a non-ancient creep will instantly kill them, summoning a fully controllable zombie in their wake for 20s
  • The ability goes on cooldown and spends mana the moment the lunge begins
  • The controllable zombie has 3 pips of health. Other stats are shared with Tombstone zombies, including Deathlust ability and damage upgrade from level 15 talent
