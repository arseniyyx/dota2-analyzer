Изменения в патче 7.42

Mistwoods Wayfarer

  • The attacks that normally ignore evasion can now be redirected to nearby trees
  • Affects the following:
  • Bloodthorn - Soul Rend
  • Monkey King Bar - Pierce
  • Maelstrom and Mjollnir - Chain Lightning
  • Serrated Shiv - Gut 'Em
  • Dark Seer's Normal Punch
  • Meepo's Earthbind
  • Drow Ranger's Marksmanship
  • Witch Doctor's Death Ward
  • Redirect Chance rescaled from 14.25 + 0.75% per level to 10 + 1% per level
  • From 15-36.75% to 11-40%

Bushwhack

  • Cast Range decreased from 1100 to 1000
  • Projectile speed decreased from 1300 to 1200
  • Radius increased from 265 to 275
  • Stun duration decreased from 1.5/1.7/1.9/2.1s to 1.4/1.6/1.8/2s

Sharpshooter

  • Max Damage decreased from 600/975/1350 to 550/875/1200

Scurry

  • No longer multiplies evasion or Mistwoods Wayfarer's redirect chance
  • Bonus Movement Speed increased from 15/20/25/30% to 20/24/28/32%

Hunter's Boomerang

  • Incoming Spell Damage Amp decreased from 20% to 15%

Таланты

  • Level 10 Talent Health increased from +175 to +200
  • Level 15 Talent Agility increased from +10 to +15
  • Level 25 Talent Sharpshooter Max Damage decreased from +400 to +350
