Изменения в патче 7.42

Afterburner

  • Now partially disabled by Break: disables the gaining of new stacks, but does not remove the effect from existing ones

Homing Missile

  • The missile is no longer immune to damage from certain types of units

Flak Cannon

  • Radius decreased from 1250 to 1050
  • Now has 50% Item Potency and can proc all attack modifiers, except for Lifesteal and Critical Damage

Call Down

  • Radius increased from 400 to 425

Side Gunner

  • Now has 75% Item Potency

Таланты

  • Level 10 Talent Health increased from +150 to +175
  • Level 15 Talent +25 Flak Cannon Damage replaced with -10s Call Down Cooldown
  • Level 25 Talent -40s Call Down Cooldown replaced with 2x Afterburner Movespeed
