Изменения в патче 7.42

Shadowraze

  • Bonus Damage per stack decreased from 35/50/65/80 to 20/40/60/80

Presence of the Dark Lord

  • Now also reduces enemy Magic Resistance by 4/6/8/10%

Requiem of Souls

  • No longer reduces enemy Magic Resistance by 5/10/15%
  • Now has a base Fear and Slow duration of 1.2/1.5/1.8s and no longer has a max duration
  • Fear and Slow duration per soul decreased from 0.6s to 0.1s
  • Aghanim's Scepter now also increases soul speed by 30%

Таланты

  • Level 10 Talent +30 Feast of Souls Attack Speed replaced with +2 Feast of Souls Collected
  • Level 15 Talent +2 Feast of Souls Souls Collected replaced with Feast of Souls Heals 25 HP Per Soul
  • Level 15 Talent +1.5 Presence Armor Reduction replaced with +1/2% Presence Armor/Resist Reduction
  • Level 20 Talent +0.2s Requiem Fear per line replaced with +0.5s Requiem Fear/Slow Duration
