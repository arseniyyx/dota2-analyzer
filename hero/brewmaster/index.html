Изменения в патче 7.42

Liquid Courage

  • No longer has a scaling effect based on health
  • Now activates at full power for 5s if you receive 400 damage from player-controlled units or Roshan over the last 7s
  • Now has an internal 15s cooldown
  • Aghanim's Shard cooldown decreased from 20s to 15s
  • The passive and active cooldowns are independent
  • Aghanim's Shard now also increases speed increase by 20%

Thunder Clap

  • Duration decreased from 4/4.25/4.5/4.75s to 3.5/3.75/4/4.25s

Cinder Brew

  • Ignite Total Damage decreased from 80/160/240/320 to 75/150/225/300

Primal Split

  • Earth's Health decreased from 1400/2300/3200/4100 to 1400/2200/3000/3800
  • Earth's Demolish damage decreased from 40/80/120/160 to 30/60/90/120
  • Fire's Damage decreased from 70/120/170/220 to 70/115/160/205
  • Fire now has Cinder Dash ability
  • Active. Vector-targeted. Can target both allies and enemies
  • Fire Brewling grabs the target and pulls it 400/500/600/700 in the selected direction, while dealing 50/100/150/200 damage to it if it's an enemy. Pull Duration: 0.5s. Cast Range: 150. Mana Cost: 50. Cooldown: 8s

Таланты

  • Level 10 Talent Brewlings Base Damage decreased from +13 to +10
