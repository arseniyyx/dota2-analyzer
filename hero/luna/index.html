Изменения в патче 7.42

Общее

  • Agility gain decreased from 3.4 to 3.2

Lunar Orbit

  • Collision Radius is no longer affected by AoE changes

Moon Glaives

  • Bounces are now full attacks with reduced damage. Lifesteal and Critical Strikes disabled on bounces
  • Now attacks have their item potency reduced with each bounce by 40%

Таланты

  • Level 25 Talent Lucent Beam Hits An Additional Target replaced with Lucent Beam Hits 2 Additional Targets
  • Targeting changed from random unit within 500 range of the primary target to 2 closest units within 500 range of the primary target
