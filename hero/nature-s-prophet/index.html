Изменения в патче 7.42

Nature's Prophet ослаб в 7.42. Почему и сборка →

Spirit of the Forest

  • Bonus damage is now granted based on trees near the target instead of trees near Nature's Prophet
  • Bonus damage is calculated when the attack projectile is created, not when it hits

Sprout

  • Damage increased from 70/130/190/250 to 70/140/210/280

Teleportation

  • Cooldown increased from 65/50/35/20s to 80/60/40/20s
  • No longer grants a 70/130/190/250 health barrier

Nature's Call

  • Treant Health rescaled from 450 to 425/450/475/500
  • Treants now have 1/2/3/4 armor
  • Treant Damage rescaled from 16/25/34/43 to 15/25/35/45
  • Treant Bonus Hero Damage rescaled from 6/10/14/18 to 5/10/15/20

Curse of the Oldgrowth

  • Ability Reworked
  • Passive. Breakable
  • Attacks of Nature's Prophet apply a stacking debuff that deals 24 magic damage per second and slows the target's move speed by 12%. Treants apply a weaker effect with 12 damage per second and 6% slow. Duration: 5s. Each enemy can have only 1 stack per attacker at a time
  • Each stack has independent duration

Таланты

  • Level 15 Talent +100 Teleportation Barrier replaced with +300 Health
  • Level 20 Talent +240 Sprout Damage replaced with +2 Nature's Call Treants (also increases radius by 50)
