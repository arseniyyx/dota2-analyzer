Изменения в патче 7.42

Общее

  • Strength gain increased from 2.6 to 2.8
  • Intelligence gain increased from 1.8 to 2.0

Ebb and Flow

  • Now fully disabled by Break

Morph

  • Aghanim's Scepter reworked
  • While Morph is active, every time Morphling casts a unit-targeted spell, 2 lesser replicants appear one after another 0.2 seconds apart, and cast it again at the random enemy within 450 radius from the target. Every echo is weaker, dealing less damage, healing less health and applying shorter buffs/debuffs than the previous one: the first one is 50% weaker, and the second one is also 50% weaker than the previous one (so its 25% of the initial values)
  • If no other enemies are found, replicants will cast the spell at the target as well
