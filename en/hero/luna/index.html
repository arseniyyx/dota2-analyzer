Luna: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.42 · Carry

Interactive guide

Luna is played mostly as Carry. The key item spike is Manta Style: built by 17:00 it wins 63% of games versus 40% later. From minute 13 it farms 19% faster than an average hero in the role. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 49.9%, pick rate 15.0%.

Power spikes

  • Ultimate: level 6 on average by 10:00.
  • Farm window: from 13 min. Farms 19% faster than average: hold lanes and the jungle, skip pointless fights.
  • Manta Style by 17:00: 63% wins, later: 40%.
  • Yasha by 14:00: 58% wins, later: 39%.

Changes in patch 7.42

General

  • Agility gain decreased from 3.4 to 3.2

Lunar Orbit

  • Collision Radius is no longer affected by AoE changes

Moon Glaives

  • Bounces are now full attacks with reduced damage. Lifesteal and Critical Strikes disabled on bounces
  • Now attacks have their item potency reduced with each bounce by 40%

Talents

  • Level 25 Talent Lucent Beam Hits An Additional Target replaced with Lucent Beam Hits 2 Additional Targets
  • Targeting changed from random unit within 500 range of the primary target to 2 closest units within 500 range of the primary target
