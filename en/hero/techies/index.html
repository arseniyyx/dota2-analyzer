Changes in patch 7.42

M.A.D.

  • Now partially disabled by Break: disables the mana regen, but not the barrel on death
  • Aghanim's Shard Max Mana as Damage increased from 40% to 45%
  • Aghanim's Shard M.A.D. now has 3 Pip Health instead of 200 Health
  • Aghanim's Shard M.A.D. is no longer invulnerable while detonating

Sticky Bomb

  • Explosion delay decreased from 2s to 1.5s

Proximity Mines

  • Mana Cost increased from 110/140/170 to 125/175/225
  • Now has a 0.25s cooldown
  • Base Charge Restore Time decreased from 15s to 14/13/12s
  • Magic Resistance Reduction decreased from 15/20/25% to 12/16/20%
  • Now has 1 Pip Health instead of 1 Health

Talents

  • Level 10 Talent -2s Proximity Mines Cooldown replaced with +150 Mana
  • Level 10 Talent +15% Magic Resistance replaced with +1 Proximity Mines Max Charges
  • Level 25 Talent -0.8s Proximity Mines Activation Delay replaced with Sticky Bomb 1.5s Stun on Explosion
