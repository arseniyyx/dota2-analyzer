Changes in patch 7.42

General

  • Strength gain increased from 2 to 2.1

Threads of Fate

  • Now applies the slow every 6s if the thread is not broken over this time
  • Thread establishing time decreased from 1.5s to 1.25s

Geminate Attack

  • Now can also activate on attacking wards
  • Additional Attacks now have +15% bonus Item Potency

Time Lapse

  • Aghanim's Scepter upgrade moved into a separate ability with shared cooldown

Talents

  • Level 10 Talent +7 Strength replaced with +5% Threads of Fate Damage per Thread
