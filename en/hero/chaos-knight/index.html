Chaos Knight: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.42 · OfflaneCarry

Interactive guide

Chaos Knight is played mostly as Offlane. The key item spike is Armlet of Mordiggian: built by 13:00 it wins 55% of games versus 43% later. Fight window: minutes 3–4, with 1.2× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in short games, best at <20 minutes. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 47.4%, pick rate 6.0%.

Changes in patch 7.42

General

  • Min Base Damage increased by 5
  • Max Base Damage decreased by 5
  • Damage at level 1 rescaled from 56-76 to 61-71
  • Damage spread decreased from 20 to 10
  • Removed Fundamental Forging Innate Ability

Ahead, Armageddon

  • New innate ability
  • Has Passive and Active components
  • Passive. Can be Broken
  • Chaos Knight gains 5 move speed for each subsequent second he's been moving without stopping, up to a maximum bonus of 20 + 2 per hero level. Stopping makes Chaos Knight lose speed 4x faster (20 speed per second). Attacking removes the entire bonus
  • Active. Point-targeted. Available only when at least 1 Chaos Knight's illusion created with active cast of Phantasm is alive
  • Global Cast Range. Mana Cost: 50. Cooldown: 30s. Cast Point: 0.4s
  • Chaos Knight prepares for up to 1s to charge towards a pointed location. The longer the preparation is, the bigger movement speed bonus is. After the preparation all Chaos Knight illusions within 1300 range are teleported to him and the charge begins. Chaos Knight and his illusions run at high speed towards the targeted point, going over terrain, destroying trees and hitting enemies on their way. Chaos Knight can slightly turn during the run, and his illusions will follow. Despite being able to turn, the charge will last as long as you'd need to reach the targeted location. The charge will stop if max duration is reached, if Chaos Knight manually cancels it, or casts any other ability. Chaos Knight keeps gained speed for 0.5s after ending the charge
  • Max Charge Speed is 2x (Chaos Knight's Movespeed with Max Bonus Speed). Reached after a full 1s preparation
  • The preparation can be stopped earlier with Stop/Hold commands to start the charge with a smaller bonus
  • Hit radius: 150 (affected by AoE bonuses). Cannot attack buildings
  • Chaos Knight gains/loses the max speed over 0.5s as the charge starts/ends
  • Only illusions under Chaos Knight's control are charging. This includes Phantasms of teammates (created with Aghanim's Scepter), Illusion Rune, and Manta Style ones
  • Cannot be cast while rooted. The charge is cancelled if Chaos Knight is rooted, stunned, hexed, feared or taunted during the preparation or mid-run. Illusions will also stop the charge if the real Chaos Knight is stopped

Chaos Bolt

  • Aghanim's Shard illusion has 25% item potency
  • Aghanim's Shard illusion no longer has 15% damage penalty and deals full damage

Phantasm

  • Phantasms have 100% item potency
