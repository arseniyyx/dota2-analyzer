Interactive guide

Chaos Knight is played mostly as Offlane. The key item spike is Armlet of Mordiggian: built by 12:00 it wins 55% of games versus 49% later. Fight window: minutes 3–4, with 1.3× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in short games, best at 30–35 minutes.
