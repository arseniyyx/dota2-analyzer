Changes in patch 7.42

General

  • Base Agility decreased from 21 to 16
  • Base Intelligence increased from 27 to 29
  • Damage at level 1 increased from 48-55 to 50-57
  • Agility gain increased from 1.3 to 1.5
  • Intelligence gain decreased from 5.2 to 4.5
  • Attack Range decreased from 630 to 600

Oblivion Savant

  • Now upgraded with Aghanim's Scepter
  • The ability gains charges. Pugna can cast any of his abilities an additional time while they're on cooldown, consuming an Oblivion Savant charge
  • Charges: 2. Charge Restore Time: 20s
  • Each ability has a 0.25s buffer before the recast is possible not to cast an ability twice by accident

Decrepify

  • Mana Cost decreased from 80 to 50/60/70/80
  • Enemy Increased Magic Damage decreased from 20/30/40/50% to 16/24/32/40%
  • Now also applies outgoing heal amplification in addition to incoming on allied targets

Nether Ward

  • Mana Cost rescaled from 80 to 75/100/125/150
  • Pip Health rescaled from 4 to 3/4/5/6
  • Ward's radius can now be seen by selecting it
  • Casting Nether Ward with an existing Ward now destroys it before creating the new one

Life Drain

  • Mana Cost decreased from 115/160/205 to 100/150/200
  • Now multiple Life Drains can be active at the same time, but Pugna can't target the same unit with multiple Life Drains
  • No longer has separate values for healing and damage, with both depending on damage/healing done to the target
  • When cast on enemy, the amount of healing for Pugna is based on the damage dealt
  • When cast on ally, Pugna takes magical damage based on the amount of heal bestowed to the target
  • As a result, Pugna will no longer take damage when the target ally has full health during the Life Drain cast
  • At the same time, Pugna will no longer be healed if Life Drain deals no damage
  • Borrowed Time, Fate's Edict, False Promise
  • Healing on allies is now increased by Pugna's Spell Amplification
  • No longer upgraded with Aghanim's Scepter

Talents

  • Level 10 Talent +2 Nether Ward Attacks to Destroy replaced with +200 Health
  • Level 15 Talent +250 Health replaced with -3s Life Drain Cooldown
  • Level 20 Talent +15% Life Drain Heal replaced with +50 Life Drain Drain Per Second
