Dragon Knight: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.42 · OfflaneMidCarry

Interactive guide

Dragon Knight is played mostly as Offlane. The key item spike is Black King Bar: built by 25:00 it wins 61% of games versus 45% later. From minute 3 it farms 9% faster than an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in short games, best at 25–30 minutes. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 51.5%, pick rate 17.4%.
