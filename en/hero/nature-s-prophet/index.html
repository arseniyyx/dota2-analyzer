Nature's Prophet: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.41 · CarryMidSoft supportOfflane

Interactive guide

Nature's Prophet is played mostly as Carry. Fight window: minutes 8–17, with 1.8× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. From minute 11 it farms 24% faster than an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in short games, best at 20–25 minutes. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 45.4%, pick rate 7.8%.
