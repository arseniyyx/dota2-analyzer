Nature's Prophet: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.41 · CarryMidSoft supportOfflane

Interactive guide

Nature's Prophet is played mostly as Carry. The key item spike is Mjollnir: built by 15:00 it wins 57% of games versus 40% later. Fight window: minutes 8–18, with 1.7× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. From minute 11 it farms 23% faster than an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in short games, best at 20–25 minutes. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 45.0%, pick rate 7.7%.
