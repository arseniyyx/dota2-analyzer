Changes in patch 7.42

Weave

  • Armor change per stack now increases by 0.1 per hero level
  • Now partially disabled by Break: disables the gaining of new stacks, but does not remove the effect from existing ones
  • Now displays a number in the overhead effect indicating how much armor Weave is adding/removing

Nothl Projection

  • Min Duration decreased from 5s to 1s
  • Travel speed on cast increased from 1200 to 1700
  • Soul Return Time decreased from 0.75s to 0.5s
  • Now upgraded with Aghanim's Shard
  • Increases cast range by 250 and the leash range by 500

Through the Realm

  • New Nothl Projection Sub-Ability granted with Aghanim's Shard
  • Active. No target. No Mana Cost. No Cooldown. No Cast Point, instant
  • Dazzle pulls his body through the Nothl Realm to his spirit and ends Nothl Projection as soon as they collide
  • The body travels at the speed of 500
  • Cannot be cast if the body is rooted or leashed
  • Can be cast if the body is stunned, but Dazzle will also be stunned for the remainder of the duration

Talents

  • Level 10 Talent +200 Poison Touch Attack Range replaced with +125 Shadow Wave Cast Range
  • Level 10 Talent +1.75 Mana Regen replaced with +15% Poison Touch Slow
  • Level 15 Talent +5s Nothl Projection Duration replaced with +50 Poison Touch DPS
  • Level 20 Talent +75 Poison Touch DPS replaced with -15s Nothl Projection Cooldown
  • Level 25 Talent +1 Weave Armor Reduction/Increase replaced with +1s Shallow Grave Duration
  • Level 25 Talent +40% Poison Touch Slow replaced with Poison Touch Undispellable
