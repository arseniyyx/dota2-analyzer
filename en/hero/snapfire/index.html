Changes in patch 7.42

Boomstick

  • Now fully disabled by Break

Lil' Shredder

  • Cooldown decreased from 24/20/16/12s to 21/18/15/12s

Mortimer Kisses

  • Damage per glob increased from 170/250/330 to 180/250/320
  • Glob no longer applies an instance of burn damage immediately. First Burn instance now occurs 0.5s after getting hit by a glob

Talents

  • Level 20 Talent 2 Firesnap Cookie Charges replaced with +1s Scatterblast Base Slow and Knockback
  • Knocks targets in the Point Blank range in the direction of the cast to the max Point Blank distance
