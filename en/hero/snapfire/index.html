Patch 7.41 · MidSoft supportHard supportOfflane

Interactive guide

Snapfire is played mostly as Mid. Fight window: minutes 6–9, with 1.4× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. From minute 3 it farms 11% faster than an average hero in the role.
