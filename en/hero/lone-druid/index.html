Lone Druid: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.41 · Carry

Interactive guide

Lone Druid is played mostly as Carry. The key item spike is Mjollnir: built by 16:00 it wins 63% of games versus 44% later. Fight window: minutes 3–4, with 1.3× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. From minute 32 it farms 37% faster than an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in long games, best at 50+ minutes. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 50.9%, pick rate 3.0%.

Power spikes

  • Ultimate: level 6 on average by 10:00.
  • Fight window: 3–4 min. 1.3× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. Look for fights and kills then.
  • Farm window: from 32 min. Farms 37% faster than average: hold lanes and the jungle, skip pointless fights.
