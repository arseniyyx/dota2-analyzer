Changes in patch 7.42

General

  • Min Base Damage decreased by 1
  • Max Base Damage increased by 5
  • Damage at level 1 rescaled from 48-52 to 47-57
  • Damage spread increased from 4 to 10

Kinetic Field

  • Now upgraded with Aghanim's Shard
  • Causes enemies that touch the Field to be struck by Electromagnetic Repulsion with 50% reduced knockback
  • The same target cannot be hit more often than once per 3s
  • Disruptor's own Electromagnetic Repulsion doesn't count and ignores this interval

Kinetic Fence

  • Mana Cost decreased from 70 to 50
  • Cooldown decreased from 14s to 12s
  • Formation time decreased from 1s to 0.5s
  • Now causes enemies that touch the Fence to be struck by Electromagnetic Repulsion with 50% reduced knockback
  • Shares strikes restriction with Kinetic Field and also ignored by Disruptor's own Electromagnetic Repulsion
