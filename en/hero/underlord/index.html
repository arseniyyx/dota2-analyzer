Changes in patch 7.42

Invading Force

  • No longer provides 3.7% + 0.3% per level damage resistance
  • Now provides physical damage barrier equal to a portion of teleporting hero's max health
  • Max Health to Barrier: 8.5% + 0.25% per Underlord's level
  • Buff Duration increased from 5s to 6s

Fiend's Gate

  • Cooldown rescaled from 110/105/100s to 115/105/95s
  • Channel duration decreased from 3.5s to 3.5/3.25/3s
  • Aghanim Scepter upgrade now creates a Pit of Malice only at the end portal
