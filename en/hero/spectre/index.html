Spectre: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.41 · Carry

Interactive guide

Spectre is played mostly as Carry. The key item spike is Manta Style: built by 25:00 it wins 67% of games versus 41% later. Fight window: minutes 9–15, with 1.6× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in long games, best at 45–50 minutes.
