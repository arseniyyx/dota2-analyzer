Night Stalker: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.41 · Offlane

Interactive guide

Night Stalker is played mostly as Offlane. The key item spike is Black King Bar: built by 26:00 it wins 65% of games versus 50% later. Fight window: minutes 8–11, with 1.4× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. From minute 8 it farms 25% faster than an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in short games, best at <20 minutes. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 53.4%, pick rate 10.2%.
