Phantom Assassin: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.41 · Carry

Interactive guide

Phantom Assassin is played mostly as Carry. The key item spike is Desolator: built by 20:00 it wins 66% of games versus 42% later. Fight window: minutes 3–4, with 1.4× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. From minute 19 it farms 10% faster than an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in long games, best at 40–45 minutes. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 49.3%, pick rate 6.7%.

  • Fight window: 3–4 min. 1.4× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. Look for fights and kills then.
