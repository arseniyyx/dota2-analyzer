Phantom Assassin: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.42 · Carry

Interactive guide

Phantom Assassin is played mostly as Carry. The key item spike is Battle Fury: built by 15:00 it wins 64% of games versus 44% later. Fight window: minutes 3–4, with 1.3× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. From minute 19 it farms 13% faster than an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in long games, best at 40–45 minutes. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 53.6%, pick rate 11.6%.

Power spikes

  • Ultimate: level 6 on average by 11:00.
  • Fight window: 3–4 min. 1.3× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. Look for fights and kills then.
  • Farm window: from 19 min. Farms 13% faster than average: hold lanes and the jungle, skip pointless fights.
  • Battle Fury by 15:00: 64% wins, later: 44%.

Changes in patch 7.42

Blur

  • Mana Cost decreased from 50 to 40
  • Bonus movement speed rescaled from 9.5% + 0.5% per level to 12% + 0.4% per level
  • Now makes Phantom Assassin invisible to enemies outside the Vanish radius
  • Has True Sight immunity
  • Getting close to revealing enemies will render Phantom Assassin visible but untargetable
  • Now the effect is removed only if the revealing enemy stays within the radius for the entire vanish buffer
  • So if an enemy hero appears, but Phantom Assassin blinks away, she preserves the effect. She used to lose it even after getting away
  • Vanish Radius increased from 500 to 600
  • Duration decreased from 30s to 20s

Immaterial

  • Evasion decreased from 25/35/45/55% to 20/30/40/50%

Talents

  • Level 10 Talent Stifling Dagger Cooldown Reduction increased from 2s to 2.5s
  • Level 10 Talent Phantom Strike Duration decreased from +0.8s to +0.5s
  • Level 15 Talent Immaterial Evasion decreased from +20% to +15%
  • Level 15 Talent Stifling Dagger Damage increased from +15% to +20%
