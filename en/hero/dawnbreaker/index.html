Dawnbreaker: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.41 · Offlane

Interactive guide

Dawnbreaker is played mostly as Offlane. The key item spike is Echo Sabre: built by 14:00 it wins 62% of games versus 48% later. Fight window: minutes 10–14, with 1.3× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. From minute 12 it farms 12% faster than an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in short games, best at 30–35 minutes. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 52.2%, pick rate 12.7%.
