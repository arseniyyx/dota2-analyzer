Death Prophet: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.41 · MidOfflane

Interactive guide

Death Prophet is played mostly as Mid. The key item spike is Consecrated Wraps: built by 14:22 it wins 56% of games versus 35% later. Fight window: minutes 4–6, with 1.6× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. From minute 3 it farms 16% faster than an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in short games, best at 20–25 minutes. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 49.1%, pick rate 5.1%.
