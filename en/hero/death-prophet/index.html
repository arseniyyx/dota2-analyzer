Death Prophet: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.42 · MidOfflaneSoft support

Interactive guide

Death Prophet is played mostly as Mid. The key item spike is Consecrated Wraps: built by 14:22 it wins 57% of games versus 37% later. Fight window: minutes 4–6, with 1.6× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. From minute 3 it farms 15% faster than an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in short games, best at <20 minutes. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 45.9%, pick rate 9.4%.

Changes in patch 7.42

General

  • Base damage decreased by 4
  • Damage at level 1 decreased from 49-59 to 45-55
  • Base Movement Speed increased from 290 to 295
  • Attack Range decreased from 600 to 500

Witchcraft

  • Movement Speed decreased from 0.5% + 0.75% per level to 0.5% per level
  • No longer provides 0.75% ability cooldown reduction per hero level
  • Now also creates a ghost whenever Death Prophet attacks an enemy. The spirit deals a portion of Death Prophet's attack damage, and then heals her for 50% of the damage dealt upon return
  • Ghost's Damage: 15% + 1% per level
  • Damage against buildings is reduced by 50%
  • Ghost's Speed: 650
  • Exorcism spirits have movespeed of 525
  • Illusions of Death Prophet also summon ghosts on attack

Crypt Swarm

  • Cooldown increased from 9/8/7/6s to 10/9/8/7s
  • Now upgraded with Aghanim's Shard
  • Grants 2 charges with 1s cooldown between uses and 10/9/8/7s charge restoration time. Crypt Swarm slows movement of affected enemies by 100% for 0.5s

Silence

  • Cooldown rescaled from 15/14/13/12s to 17/15/13/11s

Spirit Siphon

  • Base Charge Restore Time decreased from 40s to 35s
  • Now upgraded with Aghanim's Scepter instead of Aghanim's Shard
  • Additionally drains 2% of target's max health and steals 120 of their attack speed
  • Both effects pierce Debuff Immunity
  • When cast on creeps, they still lose the attack speed, but Death Prophet doesn't gain any

Exorcism

  • Cooldown decreased from 150s to 145/135/125s
  • No longer upgraded with Aghanim's Scepter

Talents

  • Level 10 Talent +75 Silence AoE replaced with +20 Damage
  • Level 15 Talent -2.5s Crypt Swarm Cooldown replaced with -8s Spirit Siphon Replenish Time
  • Level 15 Talent +50 Attack Speed replaced with +125 Silence AoE
  • Level 20 Talent +30 Spirit Siphon Damage/Heal replaced with +110 Crypt Swarm Damage
  • Level 25 Talent Deaths During Exorcism Extend Duration by 8s replaced with +1 Witchcraft Spirit
  • Level 25 Talent -22s Spirit Siphon Replenish Time replaced with Unslowable during Exorcism
