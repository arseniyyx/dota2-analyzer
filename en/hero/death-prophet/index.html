Interactive guide

Death Prophet is played mostly as Mid. The key item spike is Consecrated Wraps: built by 14:10 it wins 60% of games versus 29% later. Fight window: minutes 4–6, with 1.7× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. From minute 3 it farms 13% faster than an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in short games, best at <20 minutes. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 49.7%, pick rate 5.2%.
