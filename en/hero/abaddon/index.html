Changes in patch 7.42

Withering Mist

  • No longer has a separate health threshold at which it takes effect
  • Now always reduces the enemy's health restoration, scaling linearly with enemy's current health, with 10% at full health and max effect at no health
  • Now applies only from attack and ability damage
  • Item Damage doesn't count
  • Max Health Restoration Reduction increased from 29.5% + 0.5% per hero level to 40% + 1% per hero level

Mist Coil

  • The Self Damage portion of the ability has been replaced with a Health Cost. The value is unchanged
  • As a result, the current cost is shown on the ability icon and in the tooltip
  • Still damages the Aphotic Shield barrier on cast
  • Now also slows/increases movement speed of enemies/allies by 60% for 0.7/0.8/0.9/1s
  • Now upgraded with Aghanim's Scepter
  • Affects all units in a 400 radius around the target. Each affected enemy is attacked by Abaddon with 100% damage and 80% item potency
  • Can't apply Cleave

Curse of Avernus

  • Duration increased from 2s to 3s
  • Damage per second decreased from 15/25/35/45 to 12/20/28/36
  • Bonus Attack Speed rescaled from 10/20/30/40 to 15/20/25/30
  • Now provides 50% more attack speed to Abaddon
  • Bonus Attack Speed for Abaddon increased from 10/20/30/40 to 22.5/30/37.5/45

Borrowed Time

  • No longer upgraded with Aghanim's Scepter

Talents

  • Level 10 Talent Aphotic Shield provides +8 HP Regen replaced with +125 Mist Coil Cast Range
  • Level 15 Talents swapped sides
  • Level 15 Talent +35 Mist Coil Damage/Heal replaced with +10% Withering Mist Health Restoration Reduction
  • Level 20 Talent 100 Borrowed Time Immolation DPS replaced with +2s Borrowed Time Duration
  • Level 25 Talent 350 AoE Mist Coil replaced with 2 Aphotic Shield Charges
  • Level 25 Talent Curse of Avernus Attack Speed Bonus decreased from +65 to +50
