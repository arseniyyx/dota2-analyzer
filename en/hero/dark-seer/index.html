Dark Seer: guide, build and power spikes

Patch 7.41 · Offlane

Interactive guide

Dark Seer is played mostly as Offlane. The key item spike is Shiva's Guard: built by 18:00 it wins 60% of games versus 49% later. Fight window: minutes 31–36, with 1.3× the kills and assists of an average hero in the role. From minute 9 it farms 21% faster than an average hero in the role. Beats the average hero in long games, best at 45–50 minutes. Win rate in Divine–Immortal pubs: 51.6%, pick rate 6.3%.
