Changes in patch 7.42

Boundless Strike

  • Cooldown decreased from 24/21/18/15s to 24/20/16/12s
  • Critical Strike decreased from 120/140/160/180% to 120/130/140/150%
  • Bonus Damage increased from 20/40/60/80 to 25/50/75/100
  • Stun Duration rescaled from 0.7/0.9/1.1/1.3s to 0.75/1/1.25/1.5s
  • Aghanim's Shard now makes Monkey King jump to the target position, instead of full distance all the time

Tree Dance

  • Non-player controlled creeps no longer cause the ability to go on cooldown
  • Cast Range and jump distance increased from 900 to 1000

Talents

  • Level 10 Talent Boundless Strike Stun Duration increased from +0.2s to +0.25s
  • Level 10 Talent Tree Dance Cast Range decreased from +300 to +200
