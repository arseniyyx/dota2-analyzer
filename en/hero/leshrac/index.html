Changes in patch 7.42

General

  • Max Base Damage increased by 2
  • Damage at level 1 increased from 49-53 to 49-55
  • Base Health Regen increased by 0.25

General

  • Damage spread increased from 4 to 6

Defilement

  • AoE per Intelligence decreased from 0.7 to 0.6
  • Now fully disabled by Break

Pulse Nova

  • Damage per second decreased from 80/135/180 to 80/130/180

Nihilism

  • Ability Reworked
  • Non-targeted. Affects enemy units. Doesn't pierce Debuff Immunity. Cannot be Dispelled.
  • Mana Cost: 125. Cooldown: 25s
  • Disarms nearby enemies, while constantly applying basic dispel to them and Leshrac himself
  • Radius: 550; Duration: 4s
  • No longer applies Ethereal to Leshrac, so it can be used while Debuff Immune
  • Has no linger duration and doesn't work on invulnerable units

Talents

  • Level 20 Talent Diabolic Edict Hits An Additional Target replaced with +10 Diabolic Edict Explosions
  • Level 25 Talent +20 Diabolic Edict Explosions replaced with Diabolic Edict Hits An Additional Target
