Changes in patch 7.42

General

  • Base Health Regen decreased by 1.0
  • Removed Mana Burn Innate ability

Scarab Metabolism

  • New Innate Ability
  • Passive. Affects the wielder only. Breakable
  • After 3s without taking damage, Nyx Assassin gains bonus movement speed, and regenerates portions of max health and mana per second
  • Movement Speed: 5 + 1 per hero level
  • Max Health Regeneration: 0.35%
  • Max Mana Regeneration: 0.35%

Impale

  • Stun Duration increased from 1.1/1.4/1.7/2s to 1.25/1.5/1.75/2s

Mind Flare

  • No longer has a component of previously dealt damage
  • Max Mana as Damage decreased from 25/30/35/40% to 20/25/30/35%
  • Mana Burn Percentage increased from 9/12/15/18% to 12.5/15/17.5/20%

Vendetta

  • Bonus Damage decreased from 300/400/500 to 300/380/460

Burrow

  • No longer provides 1% max health and 1% max mana restoration
  • Now provides 3x Scarab Metabolism bonuses that are not cancelled by taking damage
  • Resulted value is unchanged
  • Burrow Bonus Cast Range decreased from 500 to 400
  • Burrow Spiked Carapace Radius decreased from 400 to 350

Talents

  • Level 15 Talent Mind Flare Cooldown Reduction decreased from 3s to 2s
  • Level 25 Talent 600 Mind Flare Radius replaced with Permanent 2x Scarab Metabolism
  • Can no longer be disabled by taking damage
