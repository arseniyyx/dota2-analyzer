Who pressed the glyph in Dota 2

Paste a match ID or a Dotabuff, OpenDota or Stratz link: you will see which players pressed the glyph of fortification, how many times and at what minute. Handy when the glyph was wasted and the chat starts arguing.

How it works

  • Glyph presses come from the parsed match replay on OpenDota. If the match is not parsed yet, the site requests parsing and waits.
  • The match ID is in the Dota client on the match page, and on Dotabuff, OpenDota and Stratz.
  • The full match breakdown (map, wards, fights and game flow) is in the player analysis.
