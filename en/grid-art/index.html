Dota 2 hero grid art

Draw a picture right in the Dota 2 hero grid: it is made of hundreds of tiny empty categories, with real zones next to it where you drag your heroes. Upload a photo (a face, a character, a logo) or paint with a brush, then download a ready hero_grid_config.json.

Make a grid art

What you can tune

  • Style: outlines or dot fill, dot count, density and the symbol.
  • Hero zones: five on the right, five at the bottom or none, with dot frames.
  • Fill the zones with meta heroes by position right away and add your own text in the corner.
  • Scale, if the picture does not fit the grid.

How to install the grid in Dota 2

  1. Close Dota 2 and download hero_grid_config.json.
  2. Put it into Steam\userdata\<Friend ID>\570\remote\cfg\ replacing the old one.
  3. Already have your own grids? Load your current file with “Add to my file…”: the new grid is added and the old ones stay.
  4. Start Dota: the grid shows up in hero pick and on the Heroes page.
