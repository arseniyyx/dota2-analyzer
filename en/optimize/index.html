Dota 2 optimisation: how to get more FPS

Dota 2 launch options, autoexec.cfg and video settings for low, mid and high-end PCs. Official settings and console commands only, game files stay untouched. In the interactive version you pick your PC, monitor refresh rate and DirectX or Vulkan, then copy the result.

Tune for my PC

Launch options

Steam → Dota 2 → Properties → Launch options:

-novid -console -map dota -dx11 +exec autoexec
  • -novid — no intro video
  • -console — enables the console
  • -map dota — preloads the map so matches open faster
  • -dx11 / -vulkan — graphics API: DirectX 11 is steadier on most PCs, Vulkan is faster on some AMD GPUs
  • +exec autoexec — runs autoexec.cfg on start

autoexec.cfg

The file goes into steamapps\common\dota 2 beta\game\dota\cfg\. Example for a 144 Hz monitor:

con_enable 1
fps_max 174
net_graph 1
dota_player_auto_repeat_right_mouse 1
echo "autoexec loaded"
  • fps_max — an FPS cap a bit above the refresh rate: smoother frames and less heat
  • net_graph 1 — FPS, ping and packet loss in the corner
  • dota_player_auto_repeat_right_mouse 1 — holding right click keeps your hero moving

What to turn off

  • Ambient creatures: birds and critters, no gameplay value
  • Vsync: adds input lag, cap FPS instead
  • Grass, cloth simulation, tree wind, atmospheric fog, ambient occlusion, high-quality water: on low and mid PCs
  • Animated hero portrait: costs FPS on weak GPUs
  • Specular, normal maps, world lighting, additive light pass: on low PCs only
