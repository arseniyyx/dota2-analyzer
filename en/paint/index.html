Dota 2 hero customisation: recolour in 3D

Recolour any of the 127 heroes in any colour: open the 3D model, turn it with the mouse, click a body part or weapon and pick a colour on the RGB wheel. Colour single parts or the whole hero at once, plus the ability icons. The D2A Mods app puts the colours into the game, and only you see them.

Open the 3D editor

How it works

  1. Pick a hero below and colour it in the editor.
  2. Copy the colour code, or open the editor straight from the D2A Mods.
  3. Press Install in the app: it recolours the hero’s textures from the game’s own files.
  4. Default hero models are recoloured. Paid Valve cosmetics are neither included nor unlocked.
