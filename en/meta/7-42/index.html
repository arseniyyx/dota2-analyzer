Dota 2 7.42 meta: the best and broken heroes of the patch

Who to play in patch 7.42: Beastmaster, Riki, Earthshaker, Bounty Hunter, Dazzle and other heroes that strong players and analysts rate highest in the first days of the patch. For each: the role, our win rate, the build and why it got stronger.

The 7.42 tab in the app Every 7.42 change

Who to play in 7.42 by role

  • Carry: Riki, Sven, Chaos Knight, Spectre, Phantom Lancer
  • Mid: Earthshaker, Storm Spirit
  • Offlane: Beastmaster, Wraith King
  • Soft support: Bounty Hunter, Pugna, Nyx Assassin
  • Hard support: Dazzle

How the game changed

  • Farming got worse: less gold from neutrals and buildings, passive gold no longer grows. Fights and kills pay more.
  • Games are faster: the glyph no longer refreshes when towers fall, and Tormentor from 15:00 gives a wave of mega creeps on every lane. Push lineups and summoners are strong.
  • Tier 1 neutral items are available from 0:00: push the wave early and grab the tokens.
  • Illusions hit harder now that they apply item effects (Item Potency), so heroes that punish illusions are valuable.
